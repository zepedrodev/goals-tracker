How it works

Three steps. The math stays in the background.

1

Name the action

Workout. Read. Practice. Set how many times you want to complete it.

2

Set the window

Pick a start and a deadline. The app shows the required pace for that window.

3

Log as you go

Tap to add an entry. Status updates immediately. Catch up before a miss turns into Urgent.

↑

Set yourself a challenge

Reframe your goals as a challenge to yourself. Trying to increase your fitness? Work out 100 times this year. Learning a new musical instrument? Practise 60 times this semester.

!

Check in on your progress

On Track, Behind or ahead of schedule — as you log progress, the app helps you know if you’re successfully working towards your goal or falling behind.

–

Skip dates adjust the plan

Working on a goal every day might not be realistic. Mark your vacations and breaks ahead of time. The app changes your required pace and status calculation, to help you meet your goal by your deadline.

▦

Progress calendar

See which days you showed up across every goal. One glance tells you if this week is real work or wishful counting.

↻

A date to catch up

Fall behind and keep logging. The app estimates when your current pace gets you back on track.

↓

Notes and history

Log an entry with an optional note. Edit or delete later. The timeline is yours, stored only on the device.

Local first

Your goals never leave the phone

There is no Goals Tracker account and no developer-operated server. Titles, notes, dates, and backups stay in the app’s private storage.

  • No ads, and nothing tracking how you use the app
  • You are in full control of your data.
  • Export and import backups yourself when you want a copy
