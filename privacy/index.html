Goals Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 September 2026

Goals Tracker (com.goalstracker.app) stores your data on your device. The developer does not operate a backend for this app and does not collect, receive, sell, or share your goal data.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter, including:

  • Goal titles, target values, colors, and display preferences
  • Start dates, target dates, and skip-date ranges and labels
  • Progress entries, including timestamps and optional notes
  • Theme and language preferences
  • Local backup files (automatic daily copies, snapshots, and manual backups) in the app’s private storage

The app also reads your device language setting locally so it can show the matching translation. That value is not sent to the developer.

Android backup

Android Auto Backup is enabled. If backup is turned on for your Google account and device, Android may copy app data (including goals, notes, dates, settings, and local backups) to Google’s backup service associated with your account. The developer cannot access that backup. You can turn device backup off in Android settings, and Google’s own policies apply to data stored in your Google account.

Export, share, and import

You can export a backup through the Android share sheet. Where that file goes (for example email, Drive, or another app) is your choice. The developer does not receive exported files.

You can import a backup JSON file you select on the device. Imported files stay on the device unless you export them again.

What this app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No transmission of your goal content to developer-operated servers

Your choices

You can edit or delete goals, notes, and local backups in the app. Uninstalling Goals Tracker removes data stored in the app’s private storage on that device. Copies you exported, and copies stored in Android backup, are not removed by uninstalling and must be deleted separately if you no longer want them.

Children

Goals Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Goals Tracker listing on Google Play, or by opening an issue at github.com/zepedrodev/goals-tracker.
